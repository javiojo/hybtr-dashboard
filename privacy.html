Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

HYBTR (operated by Axiom Creative Studios) provides the high-yield ETF tracker at hybtretfs.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights — including rights under the CAN-SPAM Act (United States) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address — only when you voluntarily subscribe to the HYBTR Monday Top 50 digest or submit a feature request via the contact form.
  • HYBTR does not currently run a site-analytics script. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party advertising trackers.

2. The HYBTR Monday Top 50 Digest

By entering your email and clicking "Subscribe Free," you expressly consent to receive the free HYBTR Weekly Top 50 email digest, sent every Monday by Axiom Creative Studios on behalf of HYBTR. The digest contains:

  • The top 50 high-yield ETFs from the US and Canada, ranked by 1-year total return
  • Yield trap alerts and notable changes in the leaderboard

Email delivery is handled by MailerLite. Your email address is stored in MailerLite's systems and is subject to MailerLite's privacy policy.

We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Unsubscribing — CAN-SPAM & CASL

Every digest email contains a clear, one-click unsubscribe link. You may unsubscribe at any time and your address will be removed promptly (within 10 business days as required by CAN-SPAM; in practice, within 24 hours). To unsubscribe manually or request deletion of your data, email hello@hybtretfs.com.

4. Cookies

HYBTR does not set any cookies on your device.

5. Data Retention

Email addresses are retained as long as a subscription is active. After unsubscription or a deletion request, your address is removed from our mailing list within 10 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

HYBTR is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@hybtretfs.com or use the Feature Request button on hybtretfs.com.
