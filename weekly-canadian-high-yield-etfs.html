Weekly-Pay and Canadian High-Yield ETFs

Target keywords: weekly dividend ETFs; Canadian high-yield ETFs. Not financial advice.

Weekly dividend ETFs in the screener

HYBTR includes dividend frequencies of weekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannual, and annual. On the dashboard, choose the Weekly frequency filter to limit the leaderboard to weekly-pay funds. Those results are still ranked by 1-year total return, not by yield or payout schedule alone.

Canadian-listed high-yield ETFs

The tracker covers Canadian-listed ETFs on the TSX as well as US-listed funds on the NYSE, NASDAQ, and CBOE. The site specifically notes popular Canadian high-income strategies from issuers such as Harvest, CI, and Purpose. Use the Canada-only country filter to view that slice of the universe. Canadian tickers in the dataset use a :CA suffix.

How to filter on HYBTR

Combine the country and frequency pills with the 1-year return filters, yield slider, and search box. You can also isolate yield traps or leveraged funds. Eligibility remains the same everywhere: at least 6 months of price history and a yield TTM of 3% or higher.

Total return still comes first

A weekly payout or a Canadian listing does not change the HYBTR ranking rule. Funds that post a negative 1-year total return are still flagged as yield traps. Review the Top 50 snapshot or the full dashboard for current figures.
