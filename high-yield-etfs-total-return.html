High-Yield ETFs Ranked by Total Return

Target keyword: high-yield ETFs ranked by total return. HYBTR is not the HYTR ETF. Not financial advice.

Why total return comes before yield

The HYBTR strategy treats total return as the primary metric. A high headline yield can coincide with a falling share price. Ranking by 1-year total return is meant to show whether a high-yield ETF actually left an investor ahead over that period, after distributions.

How HYBTR ranks funds

The leaderboard is sorted descending by 1-year total return by default. You can also sort by other columns, search by ticker or fund name, and filter by dividend frequency, country, and minimum 1-year, 6-month, or yield figures. ETFs without a 1-year total return on record show “—” for that column and sort to the bottom.

What is in the universe

HYBTR tracks 2,300+ US- and Canada-listed dividend ETFs across asset classes and payout frequencies. Coverage includes US-listed funds (NYSE, NASDAQ, CBOE) and Canadian-listed funds (TSX). Eligibility is at least 6 months of price history and a yield TTM of 3% or higher. Data is updated daily. The tracker is a personal research tool, not financial advice.

A crawlable Top 50 snapshot lists the 50 eligible funds with the highest 1-year total return in the current dataset, with the as-of date on the page. The interactive dashboard is the live screener, including caution badges for leveraged funds and yield traps.
